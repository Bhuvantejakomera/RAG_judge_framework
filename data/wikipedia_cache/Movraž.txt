Movraž (pronounced [mɔu̯ˈɾaːʃ] or [ˈmoːu̯ɾaʃ]; Italian: Valmorasa) is a small village in the City Municipality of Koper in the Littoral region of Slovenia.
The parish church in the settlement is dedicated to the Assumption of Mary.
