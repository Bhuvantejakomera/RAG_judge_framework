Beniamino Di Giacomo (Italian pronunciation: [benjaˈmiːno di ˈdʒaːkomo]; 13 November 1935 – 7 July 2026) was an Italian professional football player and manager who played as a forward. He died in Civitanova Marche on 7 July 2026, at the age of 90.

Honours
Inter Milan

Serie A: 1962–63
European Cup: 1963–64
