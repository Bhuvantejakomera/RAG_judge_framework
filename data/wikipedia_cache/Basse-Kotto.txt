Basse-Kotto ([bas ko.to], "Lower Kotto") is one of the 20 and the least populated prefecture of the Central African Republic. Its capital is Mobaye. In 2024, official estimates suggest the population reached 393,276 inhabitants.

Sub-prefectures

Kembé
