Never Give Up (Japanese: 野性の証明, Hepburn: Yasei no shōmei) is a 1978 Japanese thriller film, based on a novel by Seiichi Morimura. It was directed by Junya Sato and produced by Haruki Kadokawa. Toei Company released the film on October 7, 1978, in Japan.

Plot
A girl survives a massacre of villagers by Japanese soldiers in a remote mountain village.
