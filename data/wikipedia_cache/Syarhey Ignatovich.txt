Sergey Sergeyevich Ignatovich (Belarusian: Сяргей Сяргеевіч Ігнатовіч; Russian: Сергей Сергеевич Игнатович; born 29 June 1992) is a Belarusian professional football player who plays for Belarusian Premier League club Torpedo-BelAZ Zhodino. He has also represented and the Belarus national team and Belarus U21.
