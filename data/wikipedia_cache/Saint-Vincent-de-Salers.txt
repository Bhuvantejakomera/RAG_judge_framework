Saint-Vincent-de-Salers (French pronunciation: [sɛ̃ vɛ̃sɑ̃ də salɛʁ], literally Saint-Vincent of Salers; Occitan: Sant Vincenç de Salèrn, before 1994: Saint-Vincent) is a commune in the Cantal department in south-central France.
