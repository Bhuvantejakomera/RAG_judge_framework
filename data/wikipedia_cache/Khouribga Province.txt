Khouribga (Arabic: خريبكة) is a province of Morocco in the region of Béni Mellal-Khénifra. It has a population of 499,144 people, with a majority from Arab roots. [1]
The major cities and towns are: [2]

Bejaad
Boujniba
Boulanouare
Hattane
Khouribga
Oued Zem
