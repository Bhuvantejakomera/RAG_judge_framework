Second Chance (French: Si c'était à refaire, lit. 'If I Had to Do It All Over Again'; also known as A Second Chance) is a 1976 French drama film written, directed and produced by Claude Lelouch. It was released in France on 13 October 1976 by Les Artistes Associés.

Synopsis
After a long time in prison, a woman discovers her son aged 14, studying under a scholarship.
