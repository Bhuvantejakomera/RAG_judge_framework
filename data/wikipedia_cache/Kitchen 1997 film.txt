Kitchen (Chinese: 我愛廚房; pinyin: Wo ai chu fang) is a 1997 Hong Kong drama film directed by Yim Ho. It is a remake of the 1989 film of the same name directed by Yoshimitsu Morita; both films were based on the novel of the same name by Banana Yoshimoto. Kitchen was entered into the 47th Berlin International Film Festival.
