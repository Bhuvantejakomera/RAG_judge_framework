Azhimukham (transl. Estuary) is a 1972 Indian Malayalam film, directed by P. Vijayan. The film stars Jayabharathi, Cochin Haneefa, Bahadoor and K. P. Ummer in the lead roles. The film had musical score by M. S. Baburaj.

Soundtrack
The music was composed by M. S. Baburaj and the lyrics were written by Mankombu Gopalakrishnan and Poochakkal Shahul Hameed.
