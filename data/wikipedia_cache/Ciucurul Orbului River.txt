The Bălăneasa is a left tributary of the river Buzău in Romania. It discharges into the Buzău near Pârscov. The following villages are situated along the river Bălăneasa, from source to mouth: Brătilești, Brăești, Valea Fântânei, Bozioru, Bălănești, Cozieni, Trestia, Lunca Frumoasă and Pârscov. Its length is 31 km (19 mi) and its basin size is 190 km2 (73 sq mi).

Tributaries
The following rivers are tributaries to the river Bălăneasa:

Left: Valea Rea, Murătoarea
Right: Izvorul Călugărului, Gârla Fișicilor, Roata, Nucu
