The Arrondissement of Niort is an arrondissement of France in the Deux-Sèvres department in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine region. It has 117 communes. Its population is 199,565 (2021), and its area is 2,445.1 km2 (944.1 sq mi).

History
The arrondissement of Niort was created in 1800. At the January 2018 reorganisation of the arrondissements of Deux-Sèvres, it lost 21 communes to the arrondissement of Parthenay.
