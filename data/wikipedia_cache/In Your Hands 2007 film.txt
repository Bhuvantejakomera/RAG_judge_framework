In Your Hands (Italian: Nelle tue mani) is a 2007 Italian drama film directed by Peter Del Monte. It entered the Panorama section at the 2007 Turin International Film Festival.

Cast
Kasia Smutniak: Mavi
Marco Foschi: Teo
Luisa De Santis: Madre di Teo

Luciano Bartoli: Padre di Mavi
