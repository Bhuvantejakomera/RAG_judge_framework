Francis is a town in the province of Saskatchewan in Canada. The town is 67 km southeast of Regina and 50 km north of Weyburn at the intersection of Highway 33 and Highway 35.

Demographics
In the 2021 Census of Population conducted by Statistics Canada, Francis had a population of 182 living in 79 of its 92 total private dwellings, a change of −16.1% from its 2016 population of 217. With a land area of 0.62 km2 (0.24 sq mi), it had a population density of 293.5/km2 (760.3/sq mi) in 2021.
