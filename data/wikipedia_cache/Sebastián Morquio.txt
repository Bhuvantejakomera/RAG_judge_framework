Sebastián Darío Morquio Flores (born 22 January 1976) is a Uruguayan former footballer who played as a defender.

Teams

 Huracán 1999–2002
 Montevideo Wanderers 2002
 Uralan Elista 2003
 Atlético Rafaela 2003
 Alianza Lima 2003
 El Porvenir 2004–2005
 Universidad Católica 2005
 Aldosivi 2006
 Progreso 2007–2008
 San Martín de Mendoza 2008
 Curicó Unido 2009
 Deportivo Español 2010–2011
 Deportivo Maipú 2011–2012

Post-retirement
Morquio has served as agent for players like Leonardo Burián.
