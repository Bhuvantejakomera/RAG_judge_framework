Záblatí (German: Sablat) is a municipality and village in Prachatice District in the South Bohemian Region of the Czech Republic. It has about 300 inhabitants.
Záblatí lies approximately 5 kilometres (3 mi) south-west of Prachatice, 39 km (24 mi) west of České Budějovice, and 127 km (79 mi) south of Prague.
