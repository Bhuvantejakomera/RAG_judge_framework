Mohamad Ahmad Korhani (Arabic: محمد أحمد قرحاني; born 10 March 1981) is a Lebanese footballer who plays as a left-back and center-back for Lebanese Second Division club Salam Zgharta.

Club career
On 22 May 2018, Korhani joined Akhaa Ahli Aley from Ansar. He moved to Tripoli on 7 May 2021.
