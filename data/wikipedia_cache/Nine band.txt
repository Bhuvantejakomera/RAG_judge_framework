Nine is a Swedish Hardcore punk band, formed in Linköping in 1994. The band has released five albums and two EPs.

Band members
Johan Lindqvist – lead vocals
Robert Karlsson – bass
Karl Torstensson – drums
Oskar Ekman – guitar
Former members
