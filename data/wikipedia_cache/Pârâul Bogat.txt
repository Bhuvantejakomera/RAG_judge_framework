The Suseni (in its upper course also: Surlău) is a right tributary of the river Șușița in Romania. It discharges into the Șușița in Ursați. Its length is 24 km (15 mi) and its basin size is 68 km2 (26 sq mi).

Tributaries
The following rivers are tributaries to the river Suseni (from source to mouth):

Left: Scărișoara, Scoaba Tinicioara
Right: Pârâul Bogat, Scoaba Alunoasă, Igirosu, Valea Seacă, Clențu
