Snake Fang (Persian: دندان مار, romanized: Dandān-e Mār) is a 1990 Iranian war film directed by Masoud Kimiai. It was entered into the 41st Berlin International Film Festival where it won an Honourable Mention.
It was also nominated for the best feature film at the 1991 Chicago International Film Festival.
