The Șes is a left tributary of the river Râul Mare in Romania. It discharges into the Gura Apelor reservoir, which is drained by the Râul Mare. Its length is 23 km (14 mi) and its basin size is 75 km2 (29 sq mi).

Tributaries
The following rivers are tributaries to the river Șes:

Left: Șcheiu, Mătania, Baicu, Zeicu
Right: Pârâul Morii, Gugu, Mierla
