Sol Kyong (Korean: 설경; Korean pronunciation: [sʌl.ɡjʌŋ] or [sʌl] [kjʌŋ]; born 8 June 1990) is a North Korean judoka. She represents the sports team of the Pyongyang University of Mechanical Engineering.
