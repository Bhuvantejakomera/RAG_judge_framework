The Amusements of Private Life (Italian: I divertimenti della vita privata, French: Les amusements de la vie privée)) is a 1990 Italian-French comedy film co-written and directed by Cristina Comencini. According to the film critic Paolo Mereghetti, the film is very ambitious and has a careful attention to detail but also has some awkwardness and a confusing plot.
