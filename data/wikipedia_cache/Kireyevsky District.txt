Kireyevsky District  (Russian: Кире́евский райо́н) is an administrative district (raion), one of the twenty-three in Tula Oblast, Russia. Within the framework of municipal divisions, it is incorporated as Kireyevsky Municipal District. It is located in the center of the oblast. The area of the district is 931 square kilometers (359 sq mi). Its administrative center is the town of Kireyevsk. Population: 75,142 (2010 Census);   79,142 (2002 census); 93,073 (1989 Soviet census). The population of Kireyevsk accounts for 34.0% of the district's total population.
