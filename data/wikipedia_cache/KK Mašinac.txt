Košarkaški klub Mašinac (Serbian Cyrillic: Кошаркашки клуб Машинац, English: Mašinac Basketball Club), commonly referred to as KK Mašinac or simply Mašinac, was a men's professional basketball club based in Kraljevo, Serbia. The club used to compete in the Basketball League of Serbia B.
