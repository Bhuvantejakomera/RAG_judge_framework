The Mara (Hungarian: Mára) is a left tributary of the river Iza in Romania. It discharges into the Iza in Vadu Izei. Its length is 38 km (24 mi) and its basin area is 410 km2 (160 sq mi). Its main tributary is the Cosău.

Tributaries
The following rivers are tributaries to the river Mara:

Left: Valea Seacă, Poiana, Lazu
Right: Runc, Râuşor, Valea Mare, Breboaia, Cosău
