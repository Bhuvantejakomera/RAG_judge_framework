Ioba is one of the 45 provinces of Burkina Faso, located in its Sud-Ouest Region. In 2019 the population was 265,876. The capital of Ioba is Dano.

Departments
Ioba is divided into 8 departments:

Dano
Dissin
Guéguéré
Koper
Niégo
Oronkua
Ouessa
Zambo
