Ca-bau-kan (Chinese: 擦包看), also known as The Courtesan, is a 2002 Indonesian historical romance film, directed by Nia Dinata, and starring Niniek L. Karim, Ferry Salim and Lola Amaria. It was distributed by Kalyana Shira Film and released on February 7, 2002 in Jakarta. The film was screened at the 2003 Palm Springs International Film Festival.
