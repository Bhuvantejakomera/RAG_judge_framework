Yoshifumi Tajima (田島義文, Tajima Yoshifumi; 4 August 1918 - 10 September 2009) was a Japanese actor in film and television. A versatile actor best known for starring in numerous science fiction films produced by Toho Studios. His most iconic role is Kumayama, the greedy land developer in 1964's Mothra vs. Godzilla.
