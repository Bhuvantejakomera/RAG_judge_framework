Raduzhny Airport (Russian: Аэропорт Радужный) (IATA: RAT, ICAO: USNR) is an airport in Khanty-Mansi Autonomous Okrug, Russia 8 kilometres (5.0 mi) northwest of Raduzhny. It accommodated wide body airliners. The airport was closed in 2005.
