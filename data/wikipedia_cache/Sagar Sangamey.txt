Sagar Sangamey (Bengali: সাগর সঙ্গমে; English: The Holy Island) is a 1959 Bengali film directed by Debaki Bose. The film won the National Award for the Best Film and the Best Child Actress in 1959. The award was presented by the first President of India – Rajendra Prasad – and the then Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru. It was screened at the 9th Berlin International Film Festival.
