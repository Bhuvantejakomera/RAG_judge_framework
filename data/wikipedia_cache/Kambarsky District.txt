Kambarsky District (Russian: Камба́рский райо́н; Udmurt: Камбарка ёрос, Kambarka joros) is an administrative and municipal district (raion), one of the twenty-five in the Udmurt Republic, Russia. It is located in the southeast of the republic. The area of the district is 762.6 square kilometers (294.4 sq mi). Its administrative center is the town of Kambarka. Population: 18,106 (2010 census); 21,243 (2002 Census); 22,680 (1989 Soviet census). The population of Kambarka accounts for 60.9% of the district's total population.
