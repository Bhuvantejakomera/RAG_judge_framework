Deodara is a census town in Mandla district in the state of Madhya Pradesh, India.

Demographics

As of the 2011 Census of India, Deodara had a population of 10,008. Males constitute 52% of the population and females 48%. Deodara has an average literacy rate of 91%, higher than the national average of 74%: male literacy is 95% and, female literacy is 84%. In Deodara, 13% of the population is under 6 years of age.
