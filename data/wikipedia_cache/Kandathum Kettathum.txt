Kandathum Kettathum (English: What was seen and heard) is a 1988 Indian Malayalam-language film, directed by Balachandra Menon. The film stars Balachandra Menon, Baiju, Thilakan, Mala Aravindan and Usha in the lead roles. The film, made at a production cost of ₹15 lakh.

Plot
Krishnankutty, an educated young man, launches a studio but is betrayed by his partner Sadhanandhan and struggles to make ends meet. Out of desperation, he decides to end his financial woes with a drastic step.
