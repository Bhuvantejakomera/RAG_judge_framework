Hebbe Falls is situated at 13°32′29″N 75°43′30″E about 10 km away from the hill station Kemmangundi in Karnataka, India.  The waterfall is inside a coffee estate.  Hebbe Falls gushes from a height of 551 ft in two stages to form Dodda Hebbe (Big Falls) and Chikka Hebbe (Small Falls.)
