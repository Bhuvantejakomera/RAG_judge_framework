My House (Japanese: ぼくんち, Hepburn: Bokunchi) is a 2003 Japanese drama film directed by Junji Sakamoto, starring Arisa Mizuki. It is based on the comic of the same name by Rieko Saibara.
The film won the special jury prize at the Las Palmas de Gran Canaria International Film Festival in 2003.
