The Dream (Western Frisian: De Dream; Dutch: De Droom) is a 1985 Dutch, mostly Western Frisian-language drama film directed by Pieter Verhoeff. The film was selected as the Dutch entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 58th Academy Awards, but was not accepted as a nominee.
