Vatnestrøm is a village in Iveland Municipality in Agder county, Norway. It is along Norwegian County Road 405, on the western shore of the lake Ogge. The municipal centre of Birketveit lies about 12 kilometres (7.5 mi) northwest and the village of Oggevatn in the neighboring Birkenes Municipality lies about 6 kilometres (3.7 mi) northeast.
