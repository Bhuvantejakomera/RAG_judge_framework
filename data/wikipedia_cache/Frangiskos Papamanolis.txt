Frangkiskos Papamanolis (Greek: Φραγκίσκος Παπαμανώλης; 5 December 1936 – 2 October 2023) was a Greek Roman Catholic prelate who was the Bishop of Syros and Milos, Bishop of Santorini and Apostolic Administrator of Crete.

Biography
In 1962 Papamanolis was ordained priest of the Order of Friars Minor Capuchin and on 27 June 1974 was appointed bishop, being ordained on 20 October 1974. He died on 2 October 2023, at the age of 86.
