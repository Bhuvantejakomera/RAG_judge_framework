First Love (Japanese: はつ恋, Hepburn: Hatsukoi) is a 2000 Japanese film directed by Tetsuo Shinohara.

Cast

Masaki Nishina

Reception
It was chosen as the 3rd Best Film at the 22nd Yokohama Film Festival.
