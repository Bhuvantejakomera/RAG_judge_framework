Too Much (Little) Love (French: Trop (peu) d'amour) is a 1998 French film directed by Jacques Doillon. It was entered into the 48th Berlin International Film Festival.

Cast
Lou Doillon as Camille
Jérémie Lippmann as David
Elise Perrier as Emma
Alexia Stresi as Margot
Lambert Wilson as Paul
