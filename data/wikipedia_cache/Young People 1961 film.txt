Young People (Spanish: Los jóvenes) is a 1961 Mexican crime film written and directed by Luis Alcoriza and starring Teresa Velázquez, Julio Alemán, Adriana Roel, Rafael del Río, Dacia González and Fanny Schiller. It was entered into the 11th Berlin International Film Festival.
