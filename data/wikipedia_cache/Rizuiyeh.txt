Rizuiyeh (Persian: ريزوئيه, also Romanized as Rīzū’īyeh and Rizoo’eyeh; also known as Nīrū’īyeh, Razaviyeh, Rezu, Rīzū, and Tūkh Rāja) is a village in Shaab Jereh Rural District, Toghrol Al Jerd District, Kuhbanan County, Kerman Province, Iran. At the 2006 census, its population was 45, in 13 families.
