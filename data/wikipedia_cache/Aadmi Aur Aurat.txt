Aadmi Aur Aurat (transl. 'Man and Woman') is 1984 Hindi language telefilm directed by Tapan Sinha. The movie was telecast on Doordarshan, Government owned TV Channel of India. This film was screened in International Film Festival of India-2007. This film was remade in Bengali as Manush starring Samit Bhanja and Devika Mukherjee by the director himself.
