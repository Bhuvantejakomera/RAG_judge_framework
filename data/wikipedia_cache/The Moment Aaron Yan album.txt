The Moment (Chinese: 紀念日; pinyin: Ji Nian Ri; lit. 'The Moment') is the first  Mandarin solo LP by Aaron Yan of Taiwanese Mandopop quartet boy band Fahrenheit. It was released by HIM International Music on 19 December  2012. The Japanese edition was released on 6 March 2013 by Pony Canyon, which included the Japanese version of title track "紀念日" (The Moment). The LP consists of ten songs performed by Yan.
