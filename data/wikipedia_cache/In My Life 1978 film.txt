In My Life (Danish: Honning Måne) is a 1978 Danish drama film written and directed by Bille August (in his directorial debut). It was entered into the 11th Moscow International Film Festival. For her role as Kirsten's mother, Grethe Holmer won the Bodil Award for Best Actress in a Supporting Role.

Plot
A young man finds love and settles down, but when his young wife spirals into depression they both have decisions to make.
